How to play

    Keyboard shortcuts
    • Z–/ lower octave white keys, S D G H J L ; black keys
    • Q–P upper octave white keys, 2 3 5 6 7 9 0 black keys
    • ← / → shift octave · Space sustain
    • Enter play / pause · Esc stop
    Melody track

    If the melody sounds wrong, pick the track that carries it.
